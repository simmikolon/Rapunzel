The story

Sir Bouncealot is inspired by the drawings of a five-year-old who fell in love with Rapunzel: a princess locked in a tower by a wicked witch, a golden braid as the only way up, and a prince who comes to the rescue. Ours brought a pogo stick.

Bounce from branch to ribbon to cloud, through the deep forest, past the tower's windows and up above the treetops. Every stretch of the tower has its own weather, its own platforms and its own trouble.

  1. 400 m above the trees: clouds, lanterns, storks
  2. 250 m the tower's windows and the guards in them
  3. 120 m the deep forest, home of the squirrels
  4. 0 m the forest floor, where every climb begins

The cast

Friends

  • Sir Bouncealot

    Brave, bouncy and a little reckless. His pogo stick has a horse's head, a spring and a flame for emergencies.

  • Rapunzel

    Waits at the very top. Her golden braid hangs all the way down, and its ribbons and bows bounce you higher.

  • The stork

    A friend above the clouds. Land on his back for one big bounce and three suns.

  • Goodies

    Collect the suns. Lanterns refill your boost, hearts heal you all the way.

Foes

  • The witch

    Swoops by on her broom. Higher up she brings her scissors and goes for the braid!

  • Squirrel

    Nibbles an acorn on his branch until you come too close. Land on his head first.

  • Crow

    Flaps across the forest, beak first. Time your bounce.

  • Spider

    Dangles on her thread right where you wanted to land.

  • Guards

    Poke pikes out of the windows and patrol the balconies. The ones on balconies can be stomped.

  • Flowerpot

    Wobbles on a balcony railing, then drops when you pass below.

How to play

Every landing bounces you up. Steer left and right to find the next platform, and boost when a jump falls short: three charges, refilled by landings and lanterns.

Keyboard

  • ← → or A D steer
  • Space or a click boosts
  • P pauses, M mutes

Touch

  • Hold the left or right half of the screen to steer
  • Tap the flame to boost

Tilt

  • Switch tilt on at the title screen
  • Tilt your phone to steer, tap anywhere to boost
